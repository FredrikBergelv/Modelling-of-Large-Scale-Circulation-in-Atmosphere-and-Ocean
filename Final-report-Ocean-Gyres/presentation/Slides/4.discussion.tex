\textit{Both numerical models show western boundary currents, but only Munk theory does. Why?}
\begin{columns}[T] 
    \begin{column}{0.6\textwidth}

    \begin{itemize}
        \item The Sverdrup theory is linear, whereas the Munk theory is non-linear, and frictional force is opposite sign to the motion itself.
        \item The Munk viscosity creates a boundary layer where the frictional force can take care of mass conservation. 
        \item Friction balances continuos input of energy. \textit{Realistic}? 
        \item Gives western boundary flow in right magnitude to real Gulf Stream ($~\SI{2}{\meter\per\second}$).  
    \end{itemize}
