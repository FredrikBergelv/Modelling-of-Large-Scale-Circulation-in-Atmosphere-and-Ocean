\section*{Theory}

\begin{enumerate}
    \item State the necessary assumptions that are needed to study Rossby waves.

    \item Write down the dispersion relation of Rossby waves, the equation for the group velocity, and the phase speed. Discuss the dispersion of Rossby waves.

    \item Discuss in your own words the term dispersion.

    \item From the equations in Question 2, discuss the role of the wave number $k$ in relation to the Rossby radius $R$. Which cases can be distinguished?

    \item State the equation for $\partial h / \partial t$ with variable depth
    \[
    D = D_0 + \alpha y
    \]
    and indicate the two additional terms that need to be implemented.

    \item State the relation where $\alpha$ cancels the $\beta$-effect.

    \item Why do we have to assume reduced gravity in this laboratory?
\end{enumerate}

    \]
    Explain your choice.

Estimate the numerical zonal phase and group velocities and plot them as lines in the diagrams. Add the theoretical zonal phase and group speeds as lines in the Hovmöller diagrams.

    \item Add the numerical estimates of your three characteristic waves into the plot from Question 2. (It is acceptable to combine Questions 2 and 4 into one final figure.)
\end{enumerate}
